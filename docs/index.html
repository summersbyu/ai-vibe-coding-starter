Review the invoice terms

A term such as 2/10 net 30 means a 2 percent discount is available when payment meets the 10 day deadline; otherwise the full amount is due within 30 days. This calculator checks the amount, not the payment date.

Confirm the invoice, eligibility, deadline, and approval before making a real payment. This page is a practice calculator and does not initiate payments.
