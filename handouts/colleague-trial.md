# Vibe Coding Lab colleague trial

## Observe the path from fork to public website

This trial asks whether an unfamiliar participant can follow the student handout to take ownership of a starter, improve it with AI, review and test the result, and publish two versions. Reserve 60 to 80 minutes, with extra time for account setup. The instructor should complete the live preflight first.

Give the colleague **only the student handout**. Use their own laptop, GitHub account, and approved AI route. Do not pre-open the instructor's Codespace or publish for them. They may use the supplied reference and help instructions, but observer coaching must be recorded.

### Observation method

1. Ask the participant to say what they expect before each major action. Observe without pointing to controls or giving commands.
2. Record each checkpoint as **Unassisted**, **Used handout**, or **Observer intervention**. Note exact confusing labels or instructions and the time spent.
3. If they cannot progress for two minutes, ask what they are looking for. If you intervene, record what you did rather than counting the step as unassisted.
4. Stop for payment demands, account confusion, credential exposure, or an unrecoverable launch problem. Do not repair it with ad hoc terminal commands during the student trial.

### Ask after the exercise

1. Whose repository did you change? How did you know?
2. What is the difference between saving a file, committing, and pushing?
3. Which action made the public website update, and where does that website run?
4. What did AI change, and what did you check yourself?
5. Where did the handout lose you? Could you repeat the process without help?

### Evidence to inspect

Open the participant's fork, both commit links, and public Pages URL. Check the new comparison and a calculation case. Inspect their test notes and confirm the second sentence revision appears on the public page. Observe the final stopped status rather than relying only on a written claim.

### Suggested acceptance criteria

- Own fork and Codespace ownership are correct, with no normal-path terminal typing or observer setup intervention.
- The participant performs an AI request, explains a changed file, and independently validates the arithmetic.
- Both commits are pushed, the second revision is visible publicly, and Pages works after the Codespace stops.
- The participant distinguishes preview, local files, Git history, GitHub storage, and public hosting.

If the reference fallback was used, record Git and Pages success separately from the unfinished AI outcome. Repeated observer intervention requires revision and another fresh-start trial.
